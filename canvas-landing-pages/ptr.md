## About

This module introduces the philosophy and purpose of Test-Driven Development (TDD). Students will explore the benefits of TDD in improving code quality and development speed, compare it to other common testing strategies, and understand where TDD fits in a modern testing workflow. This prepares them to apply the TDD cycle in the next hands-on module.
